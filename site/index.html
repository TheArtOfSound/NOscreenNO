Six tools, one passphrase

Pick a passphrase you'll remember. Everything below is derived from it and never leaves your phone.

🔑

Your key

One passphrase (8+ characters) unlocks everything. There is no reset, no recovery, and no backdoor — by design.

📝

Text encryptor

Encrypt notes, keys, seed phrases, or messages into a sealed payload you can copy, share, or paste anywhere.

🗄️

Local vault

Save one encrypted note directly on the device. It stays on-device and is unreadable without your key.

📁

File locker

Encrypt and decrypt any file you choose through Android's file picker, into portable .qev files.

🛡️

Visual shield

A touch-through privacy mask over your screen — harder for shoulder-surfers and cameras to read.

🔒

Secure window

The app marks its own window secure, so it can't be screenshotted or screen-recorded by anything.

Encryption you can verify

The whole engine is open source — read exactly how your data is protected.

AES-256-GCM
Authenticated encryption: confidential and tamper-evident. Altered data refuses to decrypt.
PBKDF2 · 310,000
Your passphrase is stretched with HMAC-SHA256 over 310k iterations before it becomes a key.
Fresh salt + IV
A new random salt and IV per payload — encrypting the same text twice never looks the same.
Zero network
No analytics, no cloud, no permissions to phone home. Encryption happens entirely offline.

Install in under a minute

It's a normal direct download, so Android shows a couple of safety prompts. Here's exactly what to tap — none of it is unusual for an app installed outside the Play Store.

Download the APK

Tap Download APK (Android 8.0+). If your browser warns "this type of file can harm your device," tap Download anyway — that shows for every .apk.

Open the file

Tap the downloaded NOscreenNO.apk. If prompted, turn on Allow from this source so your browser can install it — a one-time toggle.

Past Play Protect

If Play Protect appears, tap More details → Install anyway. It flags unknown developers, not actual malware.

Open & set your key

Launch noscreeno, choose a passphrase you'll remember, and start encrypting.

Download blocked, or prefer the source? Get it directly from GitHub.

Honest about what it can't do

noscreeno encrypts the text and files you choose and locks down its own window. It cannot silently encrypt every other app or file on your phone — that needs root or device-owner control, which is an Android security boundary, not a missing button. No app distributed this way can truthfully promise otherwise.
